Tools for climbers, and for the coaches who work with them.

We build software that helps people train, notice what is happening when they climb, and talk about it with a coach. Our first product is SEE.

Product

SEE

SEE is a training and logging app for climbers, with a coach dashboard for the people who guide them. It tracks the mental side of climbing alongside technique and physical training, so a climber and a coach can see patterns instead of guessing at them.

  • Climbers log climbs and training sessions, and record how they felt at the hard moments.
  • Coaches build and assign workouts, and see a climber's progress and where the gaps are.
  • Works as an installable app on a phone, and keeps working when the signal drops.

SEE is a noticing tool, not a safety system. It helps you pay attention to your climbing. It does not replace training, judgment, or a qualified guide.

SEE is in a pilot with coaches. If you coach climbers and want to try it, write to beta@straydoglabs.com.
